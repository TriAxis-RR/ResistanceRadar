Dataset: Kathmandu wastewater ARG metagenomics from KBase

Source:
KBase Narrative: Kathmandu Wastewater ARG Metagenomics Analysis
URL visible in browser: narrative.kbase.us/narrative/256529
Created by: sunil pandey / neil347

Data visible:
Raw FASTQ files in KBase Import tab
KBase MEGAHIT Assembly objects in Data panel

Import strategy:
Download or retrieve raw FASTQ files in batches of <=10 files at a time.
Preserve paired-end structure: *_1.fastq.gz and *_2.fastq.gz must stay together.
Store under:
rr-analysis/kathmandu_kbase/raw_fastq

Pipeline plan:
FASTQ -> fastp -> Bowtie2 -> CARD/RGI -> DIAMOND/CARD -> MEGAHIT -> geNomad

Important:
Do not mix Kathmandu/KBase files with Hendriksen folders.
Do not commit raw FASTQ or large assembly outputs to Git.
Only commit scripts, sample sheets, metadata, checkpoints, and small summary tables.
